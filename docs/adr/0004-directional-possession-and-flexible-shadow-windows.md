# Directional Possession and Flexible Internal Shadow Activity Offsets

We model railway maintenance possessions as directional track closures (e.g. Up Line or Down Line independently) by default, with an optional flag for total section closure when required. Within a Joint Shadow Block, secondary Shadow Activities are permitted to have flexible internal start and end time offsets inside the Primary Block's total duration window.

Restricting all possessions to bidirectional section shutdowns unnecessarily halts parallel traffic on double-track corridors. Furthermore, forcing all multi-department tasks to strictly synchronize start and end minutes wastes department crew hours on short inspection tasks. Flexible internal offsets maximize labor utilization while protecting corridor capacity.
